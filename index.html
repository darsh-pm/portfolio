Pulse

AI feedback analysis Live

Pulse collects feedback from app reviews, support tickets, and surveys, classifies each item with AI, tracks sentiment and category trends week over week, and returns a ranked list of what to fix first and what can wait, with the evidence attached.

Problem
Feedback lands in one inbox and the loudest item wins. Nothing turns it into an ordered list of what to do.
My role
Product, design, and build, solo.
Decision
Forced the AI into a ranked output with explicit "can wait" calls instead of a summary paragraph, and attached a written rationale to every item.
Trade-off
Analysis runs on demand, not on every new item. The dashboard can lag briefly, and inference cost drops sharply.
Outcome
A live product that returns a "prioritize first" list and a "can wait" list, each item linked to its source feedback.

  • PostgreSQL
  • Groq API
  • Sentiment analysis
  • Trend analytics

Task Board

AI product planning Live

A kanban board with a planning layer. Tasks carry impact and effort scores that AI suggests and you decide. A priority matrix shows where the leverage is, and one click returns a ranked plan or a capacity-budgeted sprint with the reasoning written out.

Problem
Boards show status, not sequence. They don't say what to do right now.
My role
Product, design, and build, solo.
Decision
Kept the kanban conventional and made AI ranking something you summon, so it never reshuffles your board. Scored on a 1 to 5 impact/effort scale instead of full RICE.
Trade-off
A small, fast model over a frontier one. Rationales are occasionally blunt, but the feature is near-instant and cheap enough to use daily.
Outcome
A live planner that returns a ranked plan or a sprint inside an effort budget, and states what is deferred and why.
  • React
  • PostgreSQL
  • Groq API
  • Impact/Effort scoring
  • Sprint planning

How I work

How I approach product work.

◎

Product discovery

I start from the user's problem, not the requested feature. Requirements work on custom software taught me to look for the question behind the ask.

⇅

Prioritization

Every backlog trades impact against cost. I make that trade explicit, sequence by leverage, and give a reason when I say no.

⊕

Stakeholder alignment

Clients, engineers, and leadership rarely want the same thing on day one. I get them to one roadmap and keep them there when scope changes.
